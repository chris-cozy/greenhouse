# Magical forest background concept

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using the existing standing, resting, and terrarium spirit artwork as character/style references.

Asset: `magical-forest-v1.png`. This is a design experiment; no application styles, routes, or existing artwork were changed. The soft focus is baked into the generated image. Green glass panels are not baked into the asset and would remain real interface elements.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: A single landscape 16:9 background illustration for Greenhouse, a calm plant-care application. Output only the background artwork, not a screenshot or UI mockup.
Primary request: Create an inviting magical forest where the application's existing forest sprites live, with a deliberate soft blurred effect so it can sit behind green frosted-glass interface panels.
Input images: Images 1 and 2 are character identity and painterly-style references for the standing and resting plant spirit. Image 3 is the reference for the same spirit's glass terrarium home. These are supporting references, not pictures to reproduce as a collage.
Scene: A quiet, intimate woodland clearing framed by softly curving old tree trunks, mossy roots, ferns and a few delicate lavender bell flowers. Layered forest depth and warm, hazy light filtering through the canopy. The environment should feel inhabited and tender, not epic or mysterious.
Subjects: A few small cream-faced, leaf-crowned forest spirits living naturally amongst the moss near the outer edges and lower corners, one standing beside a root, one resting on a moss bank, and one visible within its cork-topped round terrarium nestled near another root. Preserve the reference identities: rounded cream bodies, small warm reddish-brown eyes, leafy green crowns and a single curved stem bearing a lavender bell flower. Do not turn them into humans, fairies, animals, or unrelated creatures. Keep them small within the environment, not large foreground mascots.
Composition: Wide spacious composition suitable for cropping across desktop and mobile. Reserve the broad central area and upper center for calm, dark, low-detail atmospheric space behind page content. Give the edges and corners the recognizable tree shapes and small sprites. Create gently layered depth rather than a flat green wall. No central character, no bright central focal point.
Style and palette: Cohesive hand-painted storybook illustration matching the reference artwork, watercolor and soft gouache feeling. Deep forest and charcoal shadows balanced with warm brown bark, muted honey-amber light, cream sprite faces and restrained dusty lavender flowers. Avoid a monochromatic green color wash.
Blur and lighting: Bake a tasteful soft-focus / diffused-lens blur into the entire finished artwork. Tree shapes, warm light and sprite silhouettes should remain legible, while fine leaves and textures dissolve into soft shapes. The result must look intentionally softly blurred, not crisp detailed concept art. Keep highlights restrained and the middle dark enough to receive readable UI with a tint overlay. Gentle mist and a very few soft light glows, not glitter or sparkles everywhere.
Constraints: Only the forest background. No UI panels, no glass interface mockup, no text, letters, logos, watermark, borders, repeated icon rows, or transparent checkerboard. No animation. Not photorealistic, not a glossy 3D render, not neon, not oversaturated.
